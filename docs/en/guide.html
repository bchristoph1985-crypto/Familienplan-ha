Before you start

Sternchenplan is an add-on for Home Assistant, the free smart home hub that runs in your home. There is no separate app. Everyone uses the Home Assistant app or a browser.

  • Home Assistant 2025.6 or later
  • An administrator account in Home Assistant (you have one if you set up Home Assistant)
  • For push notifications: the Home Assistant app on the phones
  • Optional: speakers, router integration, NFC tags, robot vacuum and more

Sternchenplan follows the language of each Home Assistant user. Set it to English under your profile in Home Assistant and everything appears in English.

💡 Kids don't need their own phone. A shared family tablet is enough, see Family tablet.

Install

With HACS (recommended)

HACS is the “app store” for Home Assistant add-ons. It also shows you updates later.

  1. Open HACS in Home Assistant, tap ⋮ at the top right and choose Custom repositories.
  2. Enter https://github.com/bchristoph1985-crypto/sternchenplan, type Integration, add.
  3. Search for Sternchenplan in HACS, download it and restart Home Assistant.

Manually

  1. Download sternchenplan.zip and unzip it.
  2. Copy the sternchenplan folder to config/custom_components/, for example with the File editor or Samba.
  3. Restart Home Assistant.

💡 A manual install gets no automatic updates. Download the zip again for each new version.

Setup in two stages

1. Add the integration

Where to find it: Settings › Devices & services › Add integration › Sternchenplan

  1. Enter the family name.
  2. Who belongs to the family? The people from Home Assistant are already ticked. Add kids without their own account by name.
  3. Who are the parents? Administrators are already suggested.

Then the Sternchenplan menu item appears in the sidebar. You don't need to build a dashboard.

2. Welcome wizard

The first time you open Sternchenplan, a wizard greets you with five short steps:

  1. Kids: age and favourite look for each child.
  2. Features: what you want to use, for example pet, pocket money or internet lock.
  3. Chores: suggestions by age. Tap away what doesn't fit.
  4. Rewards and pocket money: tap suggestions, choose a rate for pocket money.
  5. Photos and privacy: delete or keep photos after approval, sparing push notifications.

💡 None of this is required. Everything can be changed later under Settings.

What is where?

Kids and parents open the same Sternchenplan menu item. Who is signed in decides what you see.

🎁 RewardsRedeem rewards, savings goal, family goal, screen time and pocket money.
🙂 MePet, level, badges, history and their own look.

💡 Under Settings you can switch on help tips. Then one sentence at the top explains each tab.

Create chores

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Chores › New chore

A new chore only needs a name, who, how often and how many stars. Without a time it's due at 6 PM. Everything else is under More options:

OptionWhat it does
RepeatOnce, daily, weekly, every 2 weeks or “on demand”. Limit daily chores to weekdays, e.g. Mon–Fri only.
Take turnsWith several kids, a different one is up each time.
Minimum age“From 8”: younger kids are skipped and join in once they're old enough.
Needs approvalParents confirm first, then the stars are added.
Photo proofThe child takes a photo when ticking off. Always goes to approval.
OutdoorsIf it rains, the chore moves one day instead of becoming overdue.
StepsTurns it into a routine, e.g. a morning routine with four steps to tick off.
SymbolA picture for kids who can't read yet.
🏠 The house checks itself“Done when …” (e.g. robot vacuum docked) or “Your turn when …” (e.g. dishwasher finished).

💡 Under 📚 Templates there are 24 ready-made chores and routines by age. One tap fills in the form.

Stars and rewards

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Rewards

You decide what there is and how many stars it costs, for all kids or only some. The child redeems in the 🎁 Rewards tab. You get a push notification and approve or reject. When rejected, the stars are refunded.

  • Savings goal 🎯: the child marks a reward with “Set as goal” and sees how many stars are missing.
  • Family goal 🏰: everyone collects together, e.g. “Theme park, 500 stars”. Nobody loses any stars.
  • Bonus and deduction: under Today at the child, “⭐ Bonus or deduction”. A deduction never goes below zero and never costs a level. Set up quick buttons under Settings.
  • Levels and badges: every star moves the child forward. Spending stars doesn't cost a level.

💡 You can also approve right in the push notification with “✓ Approve”, without opening the app.

Pocket money

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Family › Pocket money

Sternchenplan keeps a pocket money account for each child. You pay out the real money in cash or by transfer and then tap Pay out.

  • Rate: e.g. 10 stars = 1 €. Kids swap themselves. Rate 0 turns swapping off.
  • Weekly allowance: a fixed amount per child on payday, Sunday by default.
  • Bookings: a bonus from grandma or a deduction, with a note in the history.

Holidays, absence and photos

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Family

  • Holiday pause: enter from–to. Nothing is due, nothing gets locked and no streak breaks during that time.
  • Absence: school trip or sick, with a stand-in. With rotation the next child is up automatically. Also quick via the ⋯ menu at the child under Today.
  • Photo gallery: only if you keep photos. Only parents can see them.

Internet lock

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Settings › 🌐 Internet lock

If chores are still overdue at lock time, Sternchenplan turns off the internet for that child's devices. As soon as everything is done, or at release time at the latest, it comes back on.

  1. Switch on Internet lock.
  2. Set Lock from and Release at the latest (default 8 PM and 5 AM).
  3. Give each child their internet switches, for example console or TV.

For this, Home Assistant needs a switch for each device's internet access. With a FRITZ!Box this works through the AVM FRITZ!Box Tools integration: enable “internet access” for the devices there. Other routers work if their Home Assistant integration offers such a switch.

💡 Better not add phones so your child stays reachable. The lock is not a complete parental control: a hotspot can get around it.

Parents can give a free pass. Absent kids and holidays are never locked.

Privacy and photos

Where to find it: Sternchenplan (sidebar) › Settings › 🔒 Privacy

  • Delete photos after approval: on (default) means the photo is gone as soon as you decide.
  • Sparing push notifications: pushes go through Google or Apple. When on, they no longer contain names, chores or photos.
  • Data of a person: download everything about a person as a file or delete it completely.

Sternchenplan runs entirely at home. No account, no cloud and no statistics.

Family tablet

Where to find it: Home Assistant: Settings › Devices & services › Sternchenplan › Configure › Family tablet

For kids without a phone: the tablet gets its own Home Assistant account. Sternchenplan shows “Who are you?” with big name tiles. The child taps their name and ticks off. After a minute it goes back to the selection. If you like, parents have to confirm everything ticked off on the tablet.

NFC tags and voice

NFC tags

Stick an NFC tag on the bin or the hamster cage. In the chore, tap Learn tag and hold your phone with the Home Assistant app to it. After that, your child just holds their phone to it.

Voice

With Assist, the Home Assistant voice assistant, Sternchenplan understands for example:

  • “I took out the trash.”
  • “What do I still have to do?”
  • “How many stars do I have?”

Updates

With HACS, Home Assistant shows new versions under Settings › Updates. Install, restart, done. Your chores, stars and settings are kept.

💡 Does the card still look old after an update? Reload the page once: in the app via ⋮ › Reload, in the browser with Ctrl + F5.

If something doesn't work

My child doesn't see any chores.

Is the child linked to their Home Assistant account? Check which account is assigned under Configure › Add family member. Without an account it only works on the family tablet.

Push notifications don't arrive.

The Home Assistant app must be installed and signed in on the phone. Sternchenplan assigns the phone through the app automatically. Also check that the app is allowed to send notifications.

The internet lock doesn't work.

Is it switched on under Settings and does the child have devices? It only locks if chores are overdue at lock time and the child isn't away. The switch must work in Home Assistant, test it once by hand.

The Sternchenplan menu item is missing.

After installing, restart Home Assistant and add the integration under Devices & services. Then reload the page.

I found a bug.

Please report it on GitHub under Issues, with the version, what you did and what happened. Questions and ideas fit better in the forum.
