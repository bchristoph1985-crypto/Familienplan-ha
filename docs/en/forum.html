Sign in

How to join.

  1. Create a GitHub account. Free at github.com/signup. If you use HACS, you probably have one already.
  2. Open the forum and tap New discussion at the top right.
  3. Choose a category, write a title and your question, post it.

The forum runs on GitHub Discussions. Why? It's free, ad-free and safe, and GitHub takes care of sign-in and spam protection.

💡 Forum posts are public and readable by anyone. Don't write anything you wouldn't pin to a public notice board.

Forum rules

So everyone feels welcome.

  1. Be friendly

    Parents here have very different levels of experience. There are no stupid questions. No mocking, no insults, no discrimination.

  2. Protect children

    No photos where children can be recognised. No full names, schools, addresses or dates of birth. A first name or “my son (9)” is plenty.

  3. No private data

    No passwords, credentials, public IP addresses, tokens or links to your Home Assistant. Black these out in logs and screenshots.

  4. Search first, then ask

    Someone may have had the same problem. Check the guide and use the forum search.

  5. Ask well

    Mention your Sternchenplan version, your Home Assistant version, what you did and what happened. A screenshot often helps.

  6. Right category

    Questions go to “Hilfe” (help), ideas to “Ideen”, your own chores, routines and automations to “Zeigt her” (show and tell). Real bugs can go straight to Issues.

  7. No advertising

    No spam, no advertising, no affiliate links. Pointing to other free projects is fine when it fits the topic.

  8. German or English

    Write whichever is easier for you. Others will help translate.

  9. Mark the answer

    If an answer helped you, mark it as the answer. Then others find it faster.

  10. Moderation

    Posts that break the rules are edited or deleted. Repeated violations lead to a ban. Report violations with “Report content” on the post.

The GitHub Community Guidelines and Terms of Service also apply. Questions about the rules: christoph.benjamin@outlook.de.

Found a bug?

Better as an issue.

A bug with clear steps to reproduce is best reported as an issue. Questions and ideas belong in the forum.
